# Global Enterprise Parental Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All full-time and regular part-time global employees  

### 1. Purpose & Scope
The Enterprise provides comprehensive paid parental leave to support new parents following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

### 2. Eligibility & Leave Entitlement
- **Primary Caregivers:** Eligible for up to 16 consecutive weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave.
- **Secondary Caregivers:** Eligible for up to 8 consecutive weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave.
- **Tenure Requirement:** Employees become eligible after completing 90 days of continuous employment.

### 3. Gradual Return-to-Work Program
Employees may opt for a phased return-to-work over a 4-week window, working 80% hours at 100% base pay to ensure smooth transition.

### 4. Application Process
Employees must submit a leave request via the HR portal at least 30 days prior to the anticipated commencement date.
