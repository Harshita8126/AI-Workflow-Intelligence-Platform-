# Annual Performance Management & Promotion Framework

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All global staff  

### 1. Performance Cycles
Performance evaluations are conducted bi-annually:
- **Mid-Year Check-in:** In July (developmental feedback, goal alignment).
- **Year-End Review:** In December (formal performance rating and compensation reviews).

### 2. Rating Scale (1 to 5)
1. **Unsatisfactory (1):** Does not meet fundamental job expectations; requires Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
2. **Needs Development (2):** Partially meets expectations; targeted skill coaching required.
3. **Consistently Meets (3):** Fully meets all performance KPIs and core deliverables.
4. **Exceeds Expectations (4):** Regularly delivers above targets and demonstrates strong initiative.
5. **Outstanding (5):** Exceptional organization-wide impact, technical leadership, and innovation.

### 3. Promotion Eligibility
Employees with a rating of 4 or 5 and at least 18 months in their current role are eligible for promotion nomination.
