# Corporate Code of Business Conduct & Ethics

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All directors, officers, employees, and contractors  

### 1. Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination
The Enterprise maintains a zero-tolerance policy against any form of discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or hostile workplace behavior based on race, gender, religion, age, or disability.

### 2. Data Protection & Confidentiality
Employees must handle proprietary company data and employee personal records in strict compliance with GDPR and corporate privacy guidelines. Never share internal data externally.

### 3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside business activities, consulting engagements, or personal investments that may conflict with their responsibilities to the Enterprise.
