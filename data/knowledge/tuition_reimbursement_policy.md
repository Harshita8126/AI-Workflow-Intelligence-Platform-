# Professional Upskilling & Tuition Assistance Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All regular full-time employees  

### 1. Annual Learning Allowance
Employees are entitled to an annual educational reimbursement allowance of up to \$5,250 USD per calendar year.

### 2. Eligible Programs
Reimbursement covers:
- Accredited university degree coursework.
- Professional certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, PMP, Scikit/PyTorch, CISSP).
- Approved online courses and bootcamps related to the employee's current role or career trajectory.

### 3. Reimbursement Conditions
- A grade of 'B' or higher, or a verified certificate of completion, must be provided.
- Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 45 days of course completion.
