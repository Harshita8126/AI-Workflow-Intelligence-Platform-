# Employee Health, Wellness & Medical Benefits

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All permanent staff and eligible dependents  

### 1. Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage
The Enterprise offers comprehensive health insurance covering 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums across PPO and HDHP options.

### 2. Mental Health & Well-being
- 12 free confidential therapy/counseling sessions per year through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP).
- \$500 annual Wellness Reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness trackers, or mental wellness apps.

### 3. Paid Sick Leave
Full-time employees accrue 10 paid sick days per year, with carry-over of up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year.
