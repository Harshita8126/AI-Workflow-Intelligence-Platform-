# Enterprise Hybrid & Remote Work Guidelines

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All salaried employees  

### 1. Hybrid Work Model
The standard operating model is a flexible hybrid schedule:
- **Core Office Days:** Tuesday and Thursday are designated collaboration days.
- **Remote Flexibility:** Up to 3 days per week remote work allowed based on team agreements.

### 2. Home Office Technology Stipend
Every new full-time employee receives a one-time \$1,000 tax-free equipment stipend for monitors, ergonomic chairs, and desk peripherals, plus a \$75/month internet allowance.

### 3. Core Working Hours & Availability
To facilitate cross-team collaboration, all employees must be reachable on Slack/Teams during core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time).
